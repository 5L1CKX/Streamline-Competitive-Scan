1. Takeaways

  • This period produced multiple vendor confirmations of agentic/agent-platform capabilities (ServiceNow, Salesforce, Netcracker, Nokia, Comarch), reinforcing that agent orchestration + governance is a mainstream telco pattern we must match.
  • IQGeo re-confirmed capture-time visual AI and deterministic auto-design (NetLux AI, Comsof) — visual-validation + algorithmic design remain competitors; none of this run's sources show a commercial LLM-driven fiber auto-design product.
  • ServiceNow expanded its governance/control story (Control Tower / AI Passport) while Salesforce emphasized long-horizon agents and outcome-based (pay-for-resolution) packaging — both give product and pricing playbooks to copy.
  • Netcracker reiterated open multi-agent platform patterns and explicit support for emerging protocols (MCP, A2A) — useful for our inter-agent protocol and billing plans.
  • Carry-forward strategic reads hold: LLM-native fiber auto-design and map/asset conversational agents remain commercially open whitespace; agentic telco stacks are accelerating toward production.
