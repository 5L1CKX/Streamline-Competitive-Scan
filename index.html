Streamline — AI Competitive Scan — 24 Aug – 31 Aug 2026

1. Takeaways

  • Nokia and Blue Planet (Ciena) now ship agentic fixed-network AI, not just assistive tools — this narrows our closed-loop whitespace but confirms demand for autonomous fiber/broadband ops. No LLM fiber design agents yet.
  • Netcracker’s Agentic AI Solution wins awards and deploys with Google Fiber, proving multi-agent orchestration + MCP/A2A is production-ready in telecom; their ROI framework is a monetization blueprint for us.
  • Salesforce Agentforce adoption is contested: partners report limited revenue, but their new pay-per-resolution Help Agent validates outcome-based pricing for agent actions.
  • IQGeo’s whitespace holds: NetLux AI (visual) and Comsof Fiber (algorithmic design) remain non-LLM; no competitor ships conversational design/asset agents on fiber data.
  • ServiceNow’s AI revenue crosses $1B with non-seat deals at 50% of new business, reinforcing consumption-based pricing as a viable model for enterprise AI.
  • Fiber-design whitespace remains unchallenged: searches return only academic LLM work (photonic-crystal fiber) or network-ops agents (Nokia/Google), not commercial LLM fiber design.
  • Render Networks hires a CTO to scale AI, signaling non-AI incumbents are investing in agentic capabilities — watch for their next moves.
