Streamline — AI for fiber management & enterprise SaaS — AI Competitive Scan — 17 Aug – 24 Aug 2026

1. Takeaways

  • No material vendor signals this period confirmed new AI capabilities in fiber design or asset agents; our LLM-based fiber design whitespace remains uncontested.
  • ServiceNow and Salesforce continue to refine agentic AI governance and monetization models, offering validated patterns for Streamline’s orchestrator, HITL trust, and consumption billing.
  • Nokia and Blue Planet shipped agentic AI for fixed networks, raising the bar for closed-loop automation but still not addressing LLM-native fiber design or asset agents.
  • IQGeo’s NetLux AI (ex-Deepomatic) holds the visual-AI lead with 30,000+ field workers and <2s photo validation, but remains trained-CV, not zero-shot VLM.
  • Netcracker’s Agentic AI Solution won TM Forum awards for Google Fiber deployment, validating MCP/A2A interoperability and per-agent KPI dashboards.
  • Bentley’s ProjectWise AI search and summaries confirm demand for NL-query on infrastructure data, a direct parallel to Streamline’s Quick Action.
  • Fiber design whitespace verdict unchanged: no commercial vendor ships LLM-based fiber design or asset agents; academic papers only.

5. Standard UX kit (implement, don't invent)

  1. One visible assistant; orchestrator routes to hidden specialized agents.
  2. Context-aware quick actions on record/grid/map: "Ask", "Summarize", prefilled prompts opening a chat with context attached.
  3. Agent transparency stream: agent narrates plan, user can stop/redirect mid-run.
  4. HITL statuses: AI fills -> per-field AI markers -> pending approval -> approve/revert.
  5. Embedded generation next to the record, not in a separate tool.
  6. Agent catalog + per-agent KPI/consumption metering.
  7. Capture-time mobile validation (photo checked as taken).
  8. Consumption pricing per agent action: simple assist cheap, multi-step xN.
