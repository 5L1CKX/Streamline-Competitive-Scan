Streamline — AI Competitive Scan — 31 Aug – 07 Sept 2026

1. Takeaways

  • This period reconfirms that large enterprise platforms are shipping agentic/agent-management primitives (ServiceNow, Salesforce, Netcracker, Nokia, Blue Planet), while the fiber-specific gap for LLM-native auto-design remains unfilled.
  • ServiceNow published product pages for AI Agents and AI Control Tower, confirming a governance + agent orchestration product posture we should mirror for sales and trust messaging.
  • Salesforce Agentforce documentation shows the same "agent builder + agent script + observability" pattern and public marketing of an outcome-based "Help Agent" (pay-per-resolution), reinforcing outcome pricing experiments as a sales lever.
  • IQGeo’s site reconfirms two commercial fiber AI vectors we must treat as baseline competitors: real-time photo validation (NetLux AI) and deterministic auto-design (Comsof Fiber).
  • Netcracker’s product page verifies an open, standards-aware agentic platform (MCP / A2A support) — a direct validation of our MCP/A2A work and a blueprint for per-agent KPI/consumption metering.
  • Blue Planet (Ciena) has shipped a configuration-and-change management product for autonomous networks; Nokia continues to embed agentic AI across fixed-broadband platforms. These moves narrow the "agent orchestration" whitespace but do not encroach on LLM-based fiber layout design.
  • Strategic read: protect and lead with LLM-native auto-design + asset agents (our differentiator); copy orchestration/governance UX and metering patterns now to reduce go-to-market friction.
